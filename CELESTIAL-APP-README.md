# ✨ Celestial Love Journey App ✨

A mystical, interactive web app to help you choose the perfect celestial moment for your love confession during your Phoenix trip (February 1-11, 2026).

## 🌙 What This App Does

This enchanting web application presents the three most magically significant dates during your Phoenix trip:

1. **February 1st - The Full Snow Moon** 🌕
   - Bold, courageous Leo energy
   - Perfect for grand declarations under the stars

2. **February 1st-2nd - Imbolc/Brigid's Day** 🕯️
   - A Greater Sabbat celebrating new beginnings
   - Features interactive candles you can "light"
   - Symbolizes love growing beneath the surface

3. **February 10th - Venus in Pisces** 💫
   - The most romantic astrological transit
   - Soul-mate energy and spiritual connection
   - Perfect for the end of your journey

## 🎯 Features

- **Live Countdown**: See exactly how much time until your magical moment
- **Interactive Cards**: Click each celestial event to reveal detailed information
- **Light Candles**: Interactive Imbolc candles you can click to light
- **Suggested Phrases**: Get inspired by witchy, romantic ways to express your love
- **Beautiful Starfield**: Twinkling stars create an immersive celestial atmosphere
- **Seal Your Choice**: Select your perfect moment and get a blessing from the cosmos
- **Comparison Table**: See all three moments side-by-side
- **Mobile Responsive**: Works beautifully on all devices

## 🚀 How to Use

### Option 1: Open Directly in Browser
Simply double-click the `celestial-love-app.html` file, and it will open in your default web browser.

### Option 2: Serve Locally
If you want to access it from your phone or share it on your local network:

```bash
# Using Python 3
python3 -m http.server 8000

# Then open: http://localhost:8000/celestial-love-app.html
```

### Option 3: Deploy Online
You can host this on:
- **GitHub Pages**: Just push to a repo and enable GitHub Pages
- **Netlify**: Drag and drop the HTML file
- **Vercel**: Deploy with one click
- Any web hosting service

## 💜 Interactive Elements

### Exploring Moments
- **Click any card** to reveal detailed information about that celestial timing
- **Read the vibe** - each moment has a different energy
- **View suggested phrases** - get inspiration for what to say

- Watch them flicker to life with animated flames
- This represents the fire festival energy of Imbolc

- After exploring, click "Choose This Sacred Moment" on your preferred date
- The app will create a special "sealed choice" section
